# Pre-PhD Advice
<hr style="border:1px solid gray">

This is a growing collection of advice for students considering a PhD in business, especially in strategy or innovation. I wrote it for the students who come to my office at BYU, but most of it applies to anyone weighing this path. Click an article to read it.

<p style="margin-bottom:1.5em;">
<a href="/pre-phd-advice/should-you-do-a-business-phd/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Should You Do a Business PhD?</a><br>
Typical timeline and pay, why the job is really about research, who thrives, and the pros and cons.
</p>

<p style="margin-bottom:1.5em;">
<a href="/pre-phd-advice/where-to-apply/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Where Should You Apply for a Strategy PhD?</a><br>
How to choose your field (e.g., Strategy vs. Organizational Behavior), how strategy programs differ across schools, a big list of programs to consider, and how to find where you fit.
</p>

<p style="margin-bottom:1.5em;">
<a href="/pre-phd-advice/successful-application/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">What Makes a Successful Business PhD Application?</a><br>
What admissions committees actually look for, how to approach letters, your statement of purpose, and interviews, plus a timeline and example application materials.
</p>

**Other perspectives.** I came across these guides after writing my own articles. They're great supplements and give you more than one viewpoint:

- <a href="https://www.lumiere-education.com/post/the-complete-guide-to-business-school-phd-applications" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Complete Guide to Business School PhD Applications</a>, by Stephen Turban and Megan Gorges. A comprehensive walkthrough of the whole process, with example CVs, statements of purpose, and interview questions.
- <a href="https://www.abhishekn.com/publications-all/a-guide-to-business-phd-applications" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">A Guide to Business PhD Applications</a>, by Abhishek Nagaraj (Berkeley Haas). A classic primer on the career and the application, written in 2010, so some specifics are dated.
